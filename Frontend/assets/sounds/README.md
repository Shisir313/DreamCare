# Sound Assets

Place your sound effect MP3 files here:

## Required Sound Files (Optional)

- `tap.mp3` - Button tap sound
- `success.mp3` - Success action sound
- `error.mp3` - Error notification sound
- `water_drop.mp3` - Water glass added sound
- `notification.mp3` - General notification sound
- `message_sent.mp3` - Voice message sent sound
- `message_received.mp3` - AI response received sound
- `achievement.mp3` - Achievement unlocked sound
- `swipe.mp3` - Swipe gesture sound
- `camera.mp3` - Camera shutter sound
- `ambient.mp3` - Background music (optional)

## Sound Specifications

- **Format**: MP3
- **Sample Rate**: 44.1 kHz recommended
- **Bit Rate**: 128-192 kbps
- **Duration**: 0.1-2 seconds (except ambient)
- **File Size**: < 50KB per sound (except ambient)

## Free Sound Resources

- **Freesound**: https://freesound.org
- **Zapsplat**: https://www.zapsplat.com
- **Mixkit**: https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/
- **SoundBible**: https://soundbible.com

## Notes

- Sounds are **optional** - app uses haptic feedback as fallback
- If sound files don't exist, the app continues without errors
- Test volumes on actual devices
- Respect user's sound preferences
